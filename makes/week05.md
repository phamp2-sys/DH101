## Reflection
Respond to this week’s reflection prompt in 200–300 words.
This GIF started with a simple idea: I wanted to make a funny GIF about my dog, who is energetic, playful, and always ready to run away whenever the door opens. I first described my dog’s personality to AI, and it generated the original concept and caption: “MY DOG ESCAPING AS SOON AS I OPEN THE DOOR.” I then changed the wording to make it sound more natural: “real footage of my loving dog with unlimited food and care escaping when I open the main door for 0.003 seconds.” That change was important because I wanted the joke to sound more like something I would actually post online, and more GenZ-like.
The repetition makes the dog’s escape more emphasized. Instead of seeing the action once, the loop makes it feel like my dog is constantly waiting for the smallest opportunity to escape. Each repetition makes the viewer expect the same chaotic behavior again. At the same time, the loop flattens the story because we never see what happens after the dog runs away, since we are still stuck in the moment of escape.
I see human choice mostly in the concept, wording, and editing. AI helped generate the initial idea and suggested using a running GIF, but I decided what felt funny and human. I searched Pinterest for the visual and used EZGIF to create the final loop. This makes authorship feel collaborative rather than completely “mine” or “AI’s.” The final GIF came from combining AI’s suggestions with my own choices about humor, wording, and remixing.
